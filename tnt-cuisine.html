01
Live Charcoal · Slow Cooked

From the Grill

Fish Choma (Whole Tilapia) SIGNATURE KSh 900

Our signature: a whole tilapia rubbed in coastal spice, charred over open coals until the skin crackles. Served whole on a bed of kachumbari with warm ugali and a wedge of fresh lime — the way it's meant to be eaten, with your hands.

Nyama Choma (Goat) KSh 950

Half a kilo of goat, salt-rubbed and slow-grilled over charcoal until the edges crisp. Comes with kachumbari, soft ugali, and a side of fiery pili-pili.

Beef Mshikaki KSh 600

Tender beef cubes marinated overnight in ginger, garlic and tamarind, then threaded onto skewers and seared over flame. Served with a tangy pili-pili dip.

Kuku Kienyeji 🔥 SPICY KSh 850

Free-range chicken slow-grilled with crushed garlic and lime, smoky on the outside, juicy at the bone. A Friday-night favourite.

"The smell alone could pull you in off the street."
02
Coastal Spice · Home Comfort

Rice & Grains

Pilau ya Nyama KSh 650

Basmati rice slow-simmered with cumin, cardamom, cinnamon and tender chunks of beef — every grain dressed in spice. Served with kachumbari and a wedge of lime.

Biryani Special KSh 750

Layered chicken biryani built from scratch: marinated chicken, fragrant rice, fried onions and saffron, finished with cool raita and kachumbari on the side.

Coconut Rice & Beans (Maharagwe ya Nazi) 🌿 VEG KSh 500

Maharagwe slow-cooked in fresh coconut milk with onion, tomato and a whisper of curry — paired with steamy coconut rice. Comfort in a bowl.

"The way grandma made it. With one secret swap."
03
The Classics · Done Right

Stews & Sides

Chapati & Beef Stew KSh 550

Flaky, layered chapati rolled fresh on the griddle, served with rich madondo — beef simmered in tomato, onion, and dhania until the gravy clings to the spoon.

Mukimo & Stew 🌿 VEG KSh 500

A central-Kenya classic: potatoes mashed with maize, beans and pumpkin leaves until rustic-green, plated with a ladle of beef gravy.

Matumbo Special KSh 450

Tripe slow-stewed with onions, tomato and a handful of dhania, until tender enough to cut with the side of a fork. For the connoisseurs.

Githeri Bowl 🌿 VEG KSh 400

Maize and beans simmered with onion and tomato, topped with creamy avocado and a swirl of chili oil. A workday lunch with backbone.

"Greens that don't apologise for being greens."

Bright · Fresh · Full of Soul

Vegetarian

Ugali Sukuma 🌿 VEG KSh 350

Sukuma wiki sautéed with onion, tomato and a touch of garlic, served alongside warm, hand-shaped ugali. The plate that built a country.

Terere & Ugali 🌿 VEG KSh 380

Amaranth greens stewed with peanut butter for nutty depth, paired with ugali. Earthy, rich and quietly addictive.

Vegan Pilau 🌿 VEG KSh 450

Spiced basmati slow-cooked with lentils and crispy fried onions — all the warmth of pilau, none of the meat.

Our Story

Food that remembers
where it came from.

Taste & Tell was born from a simple idea: the most unforgettable meals aren't fussy — they're the ones cooked low and slow, by people who love you, in pots that have seen a hundred Sundays.

We opened our doors at M Square Mall on Gitanga Road to bring those meals into the heart of Nairobi. Our nyama choma comes off real charcoal. Our pilau is layered the way coastal grandmothers taught us. Our chapati is rolled by hand, fresh every service.

We're new — but the recipes aren't. Come try a plate, then go tell someone. That's how good food has always travelled.
